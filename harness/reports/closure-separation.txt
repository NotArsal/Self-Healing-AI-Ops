THE CONCLUSION
  Closure on the reference class is at CEILING: every diagnosis,
  including every one that named a different class, had already
  cited the evidence that discriminates the detector's class.
  A metric pinned at 1.00 cannot separate anything, so evidence
  closure joins confidence and fault-name coherence as a channel
  that carries no usable uncertainty signal here.

  The reason is architectural rather than a property of the model.
  Cloud-OpsBench measures 0.38 closure because its agents must find
  their own evidence in raw telemetry and often do not. Kavach hands
  the model a bounded frame built by a deterministic extractor, so
  the relevant evidence is already in front of it and citing it is
  nearly free. The bounded frame - the design decision section 8.1 defends
  - is what saturates the metric.

  So the model's errors here are not ungrounded in the sense that
  metric was built to detect. It looked at the right evidence and
  drew the wrong conclusion, which is a harder failure to catch and
  the reason the deterministic cross-check in section 4.4 exists.

  NOT CLAIMED: that named-class closure is a signal. It differs
  between the arms, but the minority arm is n=5, which is an
  observation and not a result.
